    Talambralu

  6. ౬

    నాగవల్లి

    Nagavalli

    The couple honour family deities and ancestors with betel leaves, asking them to bless the new home.

  7. ౭

    Homam

  8. ౮

    Seven steps around the sacred fire, each one a vow for the life they share.

    అరుంధతి నక్షత్రం

  10. ౧౦

    ఒడిబియ్యం

    The bride’s lap is filled with rice, coconut, turmeric and kumkum, a blessing of plenty for her new home.
